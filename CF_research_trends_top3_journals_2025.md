## 2. METHODOLOGICAL TRENDS

### 2.1 Most Common Identification Strategies
1. **Difference-in-Differences (DID)** — The dominant method. Often combined with staggered adoption or regulatory shocks. Several papers now use **robust DID** (Callaway-Sant'Anna, Sun-Abraham).
2. **Natural Experiments / Quasi-Experiments** — Tax reforms, regulatory changes (e.g., Nonprofit Revitalization Act, fiduciary duty rules), policy shocks.
3. **Instrumental Variables** — Still used but increasingly replaced by cleaner natural experiments.
4. **Regression Discontinuity Design (RDD)** — Used in regulatory threshold settings.
5. **Structural Models** — Particularly in capital structure, investment, and dynamic contracting papers.
6. **Textual Analysis / NLP / LLM** — **Rapidly growing**. Papers using GPT/LLMs to measure corporate culture, ESG disclosure quality, policy uncertainty. (e.g., Li et al. RFS 2026 using generative AI for corporate culture)
7. **Machine Learning** — Used for prediction tasks, variable selection, and causal inference (e.g., causal forests, LASSO for controls).

### 2.2 Emerging Methods
- **Generative AI / LLMs** for measuring soft information (corporate culture, management quality, ESG commitment)
- **Blockchain/on-chain data analysis** for DeFi papers
- **Video analysis** (Hu & Ma, JoF 2025 — analyzing pitch videos)
- **Granular administrative data** (loan-level, transaction-level)

## 3. DATA TRENDS

### 3.1 Traditional Data (Still Core)
- **Compustat** (firm financials), **CRSP** (stock returns), **ExecuComp** (CEO pay)
- **DealScan** (syndicated loans), **SDC Platinum** (M&A, IPOs)
- **SEC EDGAR** (filings, proxy statements)
- **BoardEx** (director networks)

### 3.2 Growing Data Sources
- **Proprietary / Administrative Data**: Bank-level lending data, FinTech transaction records, insurance claims, hospital records, employer-employee matched data
- **Alternative Data**: Satellite imagery, social media (Twitter/X for bank runs), app usage, web traffic
- **Textual Data**: 10-K/10-Q filings, earnings calls, news articles, ESG reports — processed with NLP/LLM
- **International Data**: Increasing use of non-US data (European, Chinese, Indian markets)
- **Historical Data**: Long-run datasets going back decades or centuries
- **Blockchain Data**: On-chain transaction records for DeFi research

## 4. KEY TAKEAWAYS FOR A PhD APPLICANT

### What's Hot (Likely to Remain Active):
1. **ESG/Climate/Green Finance** — from corporate perspective (investment, disclosure, greenwashing, green bonds)
2. **FinTech & Digital Finance** — lending, payments, blockchain, AI in finance
3. **Corporate Governance with New Data/Methods** — using NLP/LLM, admin data, international settings
4. **Labor & Finance Intersection** — how corporate decisions affect workers (M&A → inequality, automation, gig economy)
5. **Private Credit & Alternative Lending** — direct lending, middle-market, non-bank finance

### What's Methodologically Expected:
- **Clean identification** (natural experiments, DID with modern robust estimators)
- **Granular data** (loan-level, transaction-level, employee-level)
- **NLP/ML as measurement tools** (not just for prediction, but to create new variables)
- **Cross-country or historical settings** for novel variation

### What's Declining:
- Pure cross-sectional OLS with standard controls (without identification strategy)
- Papers relying solely on Compustat/CRSP without novel data or identification
