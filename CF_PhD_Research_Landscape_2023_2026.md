### 2. Detailed Topic Analysis

#### 2.1 ESG / Climate / Green Finance — THE Dominant Theme

**Evolution 2023→2026:**
- **2023**: "Does ESG matter?" → Green returns decomposition, ESG-efficient frontier
- **2024**: Mandatory disclosure effects (EU NFRD), carbon emissions & cost of capital
- **2025-26**: Greenwashing detection, biodiversity finance, ESG & capital structure theory, green innovation policy, corporate ESG profiles

**Key Papers (3-year span):**

| Paper | Journal/Year | Innovation |
|-------|-------------|------------|
| Pastor, Stambaugh & Taylor, "Dissecting Green Returns" | JF 2023 | Green vs. brown return decomposition |
| "The Real Effects of Mandatory Nonfinancial Disclosure" | JFE 2024 | EU directive natural experiment |
| Duchin, Gao & Xu, "Sustainability or Greenwashing" | JoF 2025 | Pollution asset market evidence |
| Feldhütter & Pedersen, "Is Capital Structure Irrelevant with ESG Investors?" | RFS 2025 | ESG-augmented MM theory |
| Flammer, Giroux & Heal, "Biodiversity Finance" | JFE 2025 | New frontier: biodiversity risk |
| Wang, Wurgler & Zhang, "Policy Uncertainty Reduces Green Innovation" | JFE 2025 | Policy × innovation |
| Berk & van Binsbergen, "The Impact of Impact Investing" | JFE 2025 | Real effects of ESG capital |
| Li, Mai, Shen, Yang & Zhang, "Dissecting Corporate Culture Using Generative AI" | RFS 2026 | LLM applied to ESG/culture |

**Takeaway**: ESG is NOT declining. It's getting more sophisticated — moving from descriptive to causal, from broad to granular (biodiversity, specific policies), and integrating AI/NLP methods.

| Banking × Corporate | ~10% | Bank specialization, contingent liquidity |

#### 4.2 What Makes a JMP Stand Out

Based on analysis of placed candidates and published papers:

1. **Novel Data + Clean Identification**
   - Proprietary datasets (FinTech transaction data, bank internal records, health records)
   - Natural experiments (regulatory changes, policy shocks, tax reforms)
   - Not just Compustat/CRSP

2. **Timely Topics**
   - AI/FinTech, ESG/climate, private credit
   - Topics that policymakers and practitioners care about
   - Real-world relevance (SVB, ChatGPT, green transition)

3. **New Measurement**
   - Using LLMs/NLP to measure previously unmeasurable concepts (culture, sentiment, disclosure quality)
   - Machine learning for variable construction, not just prediction

4. **Intersection Papers**
   - Corporate finance × labor economics
   - Corporate finance × industrial organization
   - Corporate finance × environmental economics
   - These generate interest from multiple audiences at conferences

## PART IV: STRATEGIC RECOMMENDATIONS

### 7. If You're Choosing a PhD Topic NOW

#### 7.1 High-Potential Topic Areas (2026–2030 horizon)

| Topic | Why Hot | Risk Level |
|-------|---------|------------|
| **AI/LLM & Corporate Decisions** | Firms adopting AI for strategy, hiring, investment. LLMs transforming disclosure analysis. Huge data opportunity. | Low — growing fast |
| **Private Credit & Non-Bank Lending** | $1.7T market, minimal academic research relative to size. Regulators want answers. | Low — undersupplied |
| **Climate Transition & Corporate Adaptation** | Mandatory disclosure (SEC, EU), stranded assets, transition risk. Moving from "ESG ratings" to real effects. | Medium — crowded but evolving |
| **Corporate Finance in Emerging Markets** | Unique institutional settings, FinTech leapfrogging, governance challenges. Novel data. | Low-Medium — less competition |
| **Corporate Culture & Soft Information** | LLMs enable measurement of previously unmeasurable. Culture, trust, communication. | Low — new frontier |
| **Supply Chain Finance** | Post-COVID reshoring, geopolitical risk, trade war effects on corporate decisions. | Low — growing |
| **Healthcare × Corporate Finance** | PE in hospitals, pharma M&A, medical debt. High policy relevance. | Low — high impact |

#### 7.2 Topics That Are Becoming Crowded
- Generic ESG rating studies (need new angle)
- Bank run models without new data
- Standard DID without robust estimators
- Pure Compustat-based capital structure

#### 7.3 Methodological Investment Priorities

| Skill | Priority | Why |
|-------|----------|-----|
| **Causal Inference** (DID, RDD, IV, synthetic control) | Essential | Required for any empirical CF paper |
| **NLP / LLM** (text analysis, prompt engineering for research) | High | New measurement tool, differentiator |
| **Python/R + ML** (LASSO, random forests, causal forests) | High | Variable selection, heterogeneous effects |
| **Structural Estimation** | Medium | For mechanism papers, less common = differentiator |
| **Stata** | Still necessary | Most co-authors and referees use it |
| **Web Scraping / API** | High | Building novel datasets |

### 9. NBER Corporate Finance Program — Key Themes (2025 Report)

The NBER CF Program Report (2025) highlights three dominant research areas:
1. **Intangible Capital**: Firms' shift from physical to intangible investment; R&D spillovers; Q+ framework
2. **Corporate Governance & ESG**: Mixed evidence on green policies; greenwashing; regulatory effectiveness
3. **Banking & Interest Rate Risk**: Deposit franchise hedging; SVB-style run dynamics; monetary transmission

The report emphasizes: *"Better data are at the heart of many recent advances"* in corporate finance research.
