### C3. [major] RI compares the raw beta3, not a studentized statistic, so it is exact only under strong exchangeability and reinherits the few-effective-cluster fragility it was adopted to cure
- lenses: inference
- CLAIM: Both RI engines compare the RAW point estimate (run_randomization_inference: abs(b3p)>=abs(b3_obs); run_ri_3pairwise line 95; run_ri_flow line 81); grep for any studentizing denominator (/se, /sd, tstat) returns nothing. Permuting an un-studentized estimate is exact only under the STRONG sharp null of full outcome exchangeability across quarter-shock assignments; it is not asymptotically valid under heteroskedastic per-quarter contributions (Chung-Romano 2013; DiCiccio-Romano). So the permutation null's spread is dominated by a few high-A_t/high-|S| quarters -- the 'exact' test silently reinherits the few-effective-cluster fragility it was adopted (F3/F9) to cure, biting hardest on the fat-tailed flow outcome (kurtosis 5169.7) and cum4.
- FIX: Studentize: permute beta3/SE_perm (or a quarter-cluster score-variance-normalized statistic); A_t, C_t are already computed so the denominator is O(82) and free. Add leave-one-quarter-out on beta3 to expose whether 2018/2022 quarters alone carry each result. ~half a day. Converts the RI from 'exact under strong exchangeability' to 'asymptotically robust to heteroskedastic quarters,' the standard modern permutation guarantee.
