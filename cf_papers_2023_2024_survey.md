## Key Trend Observations (2023-2024 vs. anticipated 2025-2026)

1. **Private Equity / Private Credit** saw a surge: PE fee structures, PE in insurance, VC equilibrium models, direct lending -- reflecting the massive growth of private markets.

2. **ESG / Climate** remained prominent but showed signs of "maturation": papers moved from "does ESG matter?" to more precise questions about mandatory disclosure, green return decomposition, and whether ESG funds deliver.

3. **Corporate Governance** continued with renewed focus on shareholder activism (proxy contests, voting) and diversity (gender bias in promotions).

4. **Fintech / AI** was emerging strongly: marketplace lending, blockchain/DeFi, AI adoption by firms -- likely accelerating into 2025-2026 with LLM/GenAI papers.

5. **Internal Capital Markets / Intangibles** gained attention, reflecting the shift toward intangible-heavy firms (Crouzet-Eberly Q+ framework, star firms, knowledge production).

6. **M&A** focus shifted toward competition/antitrust angles (stealth acquisitions, pharma mergers and pricing) rather than traditional event-study approaches.

7. **Labor Mobility & Human Capital** became a cross-cutting theme: non-competes, labor mobility in acquisitions, robot adoption.

8. **Crisis Transmission** through multinationals and financial intermediaries remained relevant in the post-COVID period.
